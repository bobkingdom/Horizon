## [Independent Researcher Unveils IMGNet for Sign-Pattern Face Verification](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1urxvxh/i_built_imgnet_a_face_verification_model_that/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

An independent researcher from Indonesia built IMGNet, a face verification model that replaces cosine similarity with sliding-window sign pattern matching and reaches 96.27% on LFW using a 10.58 MB model trained on CASIA-WebFace. The approach demonstrates that sign-pattern consistency can serve as a fundamental similarity metric for embeddings, achieving near-ArcFace performance without retraining and suggesting new co-design possibilities between training objectives and inference metrics. IMGNet introduces an SW Block using multi-scale neighbor differences at prime window sizes and an IMG Sign MSE Loss based solely on sign agreement; when applied to ArcFace embeddings it scores 99.58% on LFW, only 0.24% below the cosine baseline.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/img-_- · Jul 9, 18:00

**Background**: Face verification systems typically train embeddings with losses optimized for cosine similarity, which measures global angular alignment between vectors on datasets such as LFW and CASIA-WebFace.
